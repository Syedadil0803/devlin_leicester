What Personal Data We Collect
We are committed to collecting only the personal data that is necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The personal data we may collect includes the following:
Information You Provide to Us
When you choose to contact us via phone, email or our integrated WhatsApp chat, we may collect personal information that you provide voluntarily. This can include:
  • Your name
  • Contact details such as email address or phone number
  • Address details if provided for quotations or service delivery
  • Any additional information you share as part of your enquiry
Please note that when you contact us via WhatsApp, your communication passes through WhatsApp's servers which are operated by Meta Platforms Inc. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy policy for full details of how Meta handles your data.
We do not collect or store payment details through our website as we do not currently process online payments.
Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our hosting provider and third party services. This can include:
  • Your IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Referring page and requested resource
  • Date and time of your visit
  • General location data based on IP address
Our website is hosted on servers provided by 20i (Twenty Internet Limited), a UK based hosting provider. Your data may be processed on servers managed by 20i as part of normal website operation. For full details of how 20i handles data please refer to 20i's privacy policy at 20i.com/privacy.
We use Google Search Console to monitor how our website appears in Google search results. This tool does not collect personal data from visitors to our website and does not affect your privacy in any way.
Information Collected Through Cookies
We use a small number of cookies on our website. These may collect certain technical information about your visit depending on the cookie preferences you select. Please refer to our Cookie Policy for full details of the cookies we use and how to manage your preferences.
CCTV
Our showroom premises are equipped with CCTV cameras for the purposes of security and crime prevention. CCTV recordings may capture images of visitors to our premises. Appropriate signage is displayed at our showroom to notify visitors of CCTV recording. Recordings are stored securely and retained only for as long as necessary before being deleted. Access to CCTV footage is restricted to authorised personnel only.
Lawful Basis for Processing
Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Devlin Wholesale is required to identify a lawful basis for processing your personal data. We rely on the following lawful bases depending on the nature of the interaction:
Contract:
Where you have requested a quote, engaged our services or entered into an agreement with us, we process your personal data as necessary to fulfil that contract or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract. This includes:
Processing your contact and address details to provide flooring quotations Communicating with you regarding your order or installation Arranging delivery or installation of products and services
Legitimate Interests:
We process certain personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests as a business, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. This includes:
  • Responding to enquiries you send us via phone, email or WhatsApp
  • Operating CCTV at our showrooms for security and crime prevention purposes
  • Processing technical data collected automatically by our hosting provider 20i for the purposes of maintaining website security and performance
  • Using Google Search Console to monitor and improve our website's visibility in search results
Consent:
Where we rely on your consent to process your personal data, we will always ask for your explicit agreement before doing so. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. This includes:
Sending you marketing communications such as promotions or offers Activating functional cookies such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour
Legal Obligation:
In certain circumstances we may be required to process your personal data to comply with a legal obligation. This includes:
Maintaining records as required by HMRC or other regulatory bodies Responding to lawful requests from law enforcement or government authorities Storing your cookie consent preferences as required under PECR and UK GDPR
Marketing
Send you relevant marketing communications about our products, services and promotions
We will only ever send you marketing communications if you have explicitly opted in to receive them. You can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time by contacting us at [Email Address] or by following the unsubscribe link in any marketing email.
Cookie Related Purposes
Process technical data collected through strictly necessary cookies to ensure our website functions correctly
Activate functional cookies such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour only where you have given your explicit consent
Legal and Compliance Purposes
Comply with legal obligations including HMRC requirements and regulatory requests
Respond to lawful requests from law enforcement or government authorities where required
How We Use Your Information
Devlin Wholesale uses the personal data we collect only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable data protection law. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.
Specifically we may use your personal data to:
  • Responding to You
  • Respond to your enquiries or requests for information
  • Provide you with flooring quotes or arrange service appointments
  • Coordinate delivery or fitting services at your property
  • Keep a record of past communications for customer service purposes
Running Our Business
Maintain and improve the content and performance of our website at devlinwholesale.co.uk Monitor how our website appears in Google search results through Google Search Console Ensure the security and proper operation of our website through data processed by our hosting provider 20i
Security
Operate CCTV at our showroom premises for the purposes of security and crime prevention
Protect the safety of our staff, customers and premises
Marketing Communications
Devlin Wholesale may send you marketing communications about our flooring products, services, promotions and company news. We will only ever contact you for marketing purposes in the following circumstances:
Where You Have Given Consent
Where you have explicitly opted in to receive marketing communications from us we may contact you by:
  • Email
  • Phone
  • SMS or messaging services including WhatsApp
Existing Customers
Where you are an existing customer and have purchased products or services from us, we may contact you with information about similar products or services we offer. You can opt out of these communications at any time.
Marketing Preferences
If you have given us your permission, we may occasionally send you updates about our products, services and promotions by phone, email or messaging services. You can opt out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time by contacting us directly or using the unsubscribe link provided in any of our communications. We will honour all opt out requests promptly.
We will never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
How Long We Retain Your Data
Devlin Wholesale retains personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.
We will not retain your personal data for longer than necessary for the purposes set out in this policy. Where we are legally required to retain data — for example by HMRC or other regulatory bodies — we will retain it for the minimum period required by law.
In practice this means:
  • Enquiries and quotes that do not result in a contract will be retained for a reasonable period before being securely deleted.
  • CCTV footage is retained for a short period only and overwritten on a rolling basis unless required in connection with a security or legal matter.
  • Marketing contact details are retained until you withdraw your consent after which they are promptly removed.
  • Records of cookie consent preferences are retained for a reasonable period in line with standard practice.
When your personal data is no longer required we will ensure it is securely deleted or anonymised so that you can no longer be identified from it.
If you would like us to delete your personal data sooner please refer to your rights under the Your Rights Under UK GDPR section of this policy.
Cookies
Devlin Wholesale uses a small number of cookies and similar browser storage technologies on our website.
We use local storage to remember your cookie consent preferences so you are not asked repeatedly on every visit.
We do not currently use Google Analytics or any other website analytics service. Should this change in the future we will update this policy and our cookie consent settings accordingly, and where required seek your explicit consent before any analytics tools are activated.
Some optional third party content embedded on our website — such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour — may use cookies or similar technologies when activated. These are only loaded with your explicit consent.
For full details of the cookies we use, how they work and how to manage your preferences please refer to our Cookie Policy.
Third-Party Website Services
Our virtual showroom is provided by Matterport. Loading the Matterport tour connects your browser to Matterport and may allow Matterport to process technical usage data and use cookies or similar technologies. Our Google coverage map is not loaded until you choose to load it; once loaded, Google may process technical usage data and use cookies or similar technologies. Third-party providers may process data outside the UK under the safeguards described in their own privacy notices.

Links to Google, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and other external websites do not contact those services merely because the links appear on our page. If you follow a link, the destination provider will process your visit under its own privacy policy.
How We Store Your Information
We store personal information provided to us through phone, email, or WhatsApp communications in a secure and confidential manner. As we currently operate using offline records, any paper-based or digital files are held securely and accessed only by authorised personnel. We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose it was collected for, or to comply with legal and business requirements.
Disclosure of Information
We may disclose your personal data to third parties who help us provide services to you. These may include delivery providers, flooring fitters, or other subcontractors who assist in carrying out work on our behalf. We ensure that these parties handle your data in accordance with data protection law and only use it for the specific purposes required. We do not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties.
