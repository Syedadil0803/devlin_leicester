Devlin Wholesale - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Who We Are

Devlin Wholesale is a family run flooring and carpet business operating multiple showroom locations across the United Kingdom. We offer a wide range of flooring products, expert advice and professional installation services.

This website is operated by:

  • Awesome Flooring Limited trading as Devlin Wholesale - Groby Leicester Showroom, Laundon Way, Groby, Leicester LE6 0YG

For the purposes of UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Awesome Flooring Limited trading as Devlin Wholesale is a Data Controller responsible for your personal data collected through this website at devlinleicesterlvtflooring.co.uk.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently, securely and in accordance with applicable data protection law including UK GDPR, EU GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your data through this website please refer to the contact details available on this website.

What Personal Data We Collect

We are committed to collecting only the personal data that is necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The personal data we may collect through our website includes the following:

Information You Provide to Us

When you choose to contact us via phone, email or our integrated WhatsApp chat, we may collect personal information that you provide voluntarily. This can include:

  • Your name
  • Contact details such as email address or phone number
  • Address details if provided for quotations or service delivery
  • Any additional information you share as part of your enquiry

Please note that when you contact us via WhatsApp, your communication passes through WhatsApp's servers which are operated by Meta Platforms Inc. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy policy for full details of how Meta handles your data.

We do not collect or store payment details through our website as we do not currently process online payments.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website at devlinleicesterlvtflooring.co.uk, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our hosting provider and third party services. This can include:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser and device type
  • Referring page and requested resource
  • Date and time of your visit
  • General location data based on IP address

Our website is hosted on infrastructure provided by 20i (Twenty Internet Limited), a UK based hosting provider. As part of normal website operation your data may be processed on servers managed within the 20i infrastructure. For full details of how 20i handles data please refer to 20i's privacy policy at 20i.com/privacy.

We use Google Search Console to monitor how our website appears in Google search results. This tool does not collect personal data from visitors to our website and does not affect your privacy in any way.

Information Collected Through Cookies

We use a small number of cookies on our website. These may collect certain technical information about your visit depending on the cookie preferences you select. Please refer to our Cookie Policy for full details of the cookies we use and how to manage your preferences.

Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK GDPR and EU GDPR, Devlin Wholesale is required to identify a lawful basis for processing your personal data collected through this website. We rely on the following lawful bases:

Legitimate Interests

We process certain personal data on the basis of our legitimate interests as a business, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. This includes:

  • Responding to enquiries you send us via phone, email or WhatsApp through our website
  • Processing technical data collected automatically by our hosting provider 20i for the purposes of maintaining website security and performance
  • Using Google Search Console to monitor and improve our website's visibility in search results

Consent

Where we rely on your consent to process your personal data, we will always ask for your explicit agreement before doing so. You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. This includes:

  • Sending you marketing communications such as promotions or offers
  • Activating functional cookies such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour

Legal Obligation

In certain circumstances we may be required to process your personal data to comply with a legal obligation. This includes:

  • Responding to lawful requests from law enforcement or government authorities
  • Storing your cookie consent preferences as required under PECR and UK GDPR
How We Use Your Information

Devlin Wholesale uses the personal data we collect only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with applicable data protection law. We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

Specifically, we may use your personal data to:

Responding to You

  • Respond to your enquiries or requests for information submitted through our website
  • Provide you with flooring quotes or arrange service appointments at our Groby Leicester showrooms
  • Coordinate delivery or fitting services at your property
  • Keep a record of past communications for customer service purposes

Running Our Website

  • Maintain and improve the content and performance of our website at devlinleicesterlvtflooring.co.uk
  • Monitor how our website appears in Google search results through Google Search Console
  • Ensure the security and proper operation of our website through data processed by our hosting provider 20i

Marketing

  • Send you relevant marketing communications about our products, services and promotions

We will only ever send you marketing communications if you have explicitly opted in to receive them. You can opt out at any time by contacting us directly or using the unsubscribe link in any of our communications.

Cookie Related Purposes

  • Process technical data collected through strictly necessary cookies to ensure our website functions correctly
  • Activate functional cookies such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour only where you have given your explicit consent
Marketing Communications

Devlin Wholesale Groby, Leicester Showroom may send you marketing communications about our flooring products, services, promotions and showroom news. We will only ever contact you for marketing purposes in the following circumstances:

Where You Have Given Consent

Where you have explicitly opted in to receive marketing communications from us, we may contact you by:

  • Email
  • Phone
  • SMS or messaging services including WhatsApp

Existing Customers

Where you are an existing customer and have purchased products or services from us, we may contact you with information about similar products or services we offer. You can opt out of these communications at any time.

Marketing Preferences

If you have given us your permission, we may occasionally send you updates about our products, services and promotions by phone, email or messaging services.

You can opt out of receiving marketing communications from us at any time by contacting us directly or using the unsubscribe link provided in any of our communications. We will honour all opt out requests promptly.

We will never sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

How Long We Retain Your Data

Devlin Wholesale retains personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

We will not retain your personal data for longer than necessary for the purposes set out in this policy. Where we are legally required to retain data, we will retain it for the minimum period required by law.

In practice this means:

  • Enquiries submitted through our website that do not result in an appointment or service will be retained for a reasonable period before being securely deleted
  • Communications received through WhatsApp are retained for a reasonable period to allow us to respond to and manage your enquiry, after which they are deleted. Please note that WhatsApp message history is also subject to Meta's own data retention policies which are outside our control
  • Marketing contact details are retained until you withdraw your consent after which they are promptly removed
  • Records of your cookie consent preferences are retained for a reasonable period in line with standard practice
  • Technical data collected automatically by our hosting provider is retained in line with 20i's own data retention practices

When your personal data is no longer required we will ensure it is securely deleted or anonymised so that you can no longer be identified from it.

If you would like us to delete your personal data sooner, please refer to your rights under the Your Rights Under UK GDPR section of this policy.

Cookies

Devlin Wholesale Groby, Leicester Showroom uses a small number of cookies and similar browser storage technologies on our website.

We use local storage to remember your cookie consent preferences so you are not asked repeatedly on every visit.

We do not currently use Google Analytics or any other website analytics service. Should this change in the future we will update this policy and our cookie consent settings accordingly, and where required seek your explicit consent before any analytics tools are activated.

Some optional third-party content embedded on our website - such as Google Maps and our virtual showroom tour - may use cookies or similar technologies when activated. These are only loaded with your explicit consent.

For full details of the cookies we use, how they work and how to manage your preferences please refer to our Cookie Policy.

We do not transfer your personal data outside of the United Kingdom without ensuring appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK GDPR.

Your Rights Under UK GDPR

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access
You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. This is known as a Subject Access Request (SAR).

Right to Rectification
You have the right to request that we correct any personal data we hold about you that is inaccurate or incomplete.

Right to Erasure
You have the right to request that we delete your personal data in certain circumstances - for example where the data is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected for.

Right to Restriction of Processing
You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances - for example where you contest the accuracy of the data we hold.

Right to Data Portability
Where we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, you have the right to request that we provide your data to you in a structured, commonly used and machine readable format.

Right to Object
You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data where we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis for processing.

Right to Withdraw Consent
Where we rely on your consent to process your personal data - for example for marketing communications or functional cookies - you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing your consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before your withdrawal.

How to Exercise Your Rights
If you would like to exercise any of the above rights, please refer to the contact details available on this website. We take all privacy related requests seriously and will respond to you as promptly as possible in accordance with our obligations under UK GDPR.

Where a request is particularly complex we will keep you informed of our progress and expected response timeframe.

Right to Lodge a Complaint
If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) - the UK's data protection regulator.

You can contact the ICO at:
Website: ico.org.uk
Telephone: 0303 123 1113
Post: Information Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF

We would however appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you contact the ICO — please refer to the contact details available on this website.

Contact Us

We aim to respond to all privacy related enquiries promptly and in accordance with our obligations under UK GDPR.
